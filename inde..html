All readings, asset details and dates are simulated for demonstration. In a real deployment, ESP32 sensor nodes publish vibration, temperature and current over MQTT and this screen reads them live. Health index = 100 minus weighted drift from baseline: vibration 50%, temperature 30%, current 20%. Alarm priorities follow the common acknowledge, return-to-normal, clear cycle used in process plants.
